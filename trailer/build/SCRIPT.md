# THREE BLIND MICE: SEA-ALL — teaser trailer (29.0 s)

## Concept
Three mouse navigators, jacked into the Sea-All, chase a trail to Port Fauxlio and the fabled
Inggrade Passage. The trail is bait laid by Digitail Rott. The trailer never explains; it withholds
what happens to Bravo, what Rott is, and where the mice are. Then a pull-back through curved glass
shows the whole seascape is a snow globe on a student's desk, the three mice are
harnessed in its base, and the globe goes over the edge. PLUNK.

The names on the desk (SEMESTER SCHEDULE, GRADE POINT AVERAGE, PORTFOLIO) re-read the voyage
that came before: Sea of Mestre, Point Gray D'Average, Port Fauxlio.

## Two irreconcilable realities
| | THE SEA-ALL (jacked in) | THE PHYSICAL WORLD |
|---|---|---|
| Palette | navy/teal night, amber lamps, cyan overlay glow | graphite greys on paper; only colour = the globe's world + one yellow highlighter stroke |
| Line / texture | oil impasto + ink; overlay: hard square tiles, 1px grid, wireframe cuboids | pencil hatching, paper tooth, smudge, construction lines |
| Light | many warm point sources, volumetric beam, emissive overlay, bloom | one harsh desk lamp, hard shadow, no bloom |
| Lens | wide, glossy: bokeh, anamorphic flare, chromatic fringing, halation | macro, razor-thin focus, no flare, no fringing |
| Motion | continuous 30 fps glides, sub-frame motion blur | stepped 12 fps (on twos) + line boil, handheld judder, gravity |
| Sound | huge, wide, reverberant score + shimmer; voices through a crackling channel | dry, mono, close: clock, rain on glass, pump, chains, tinny music box, breath, squeak |

Leitmotif: "Three Blind Mice" (E–D–C). In the Sea-All it is minor, orchestral, and drowned in reverb.
In the physical world it plays as the source it always was: a small major-key music box in the globe's base.
